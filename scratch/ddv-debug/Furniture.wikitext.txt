'''Furniture''' items are decorative items that can be placed in the players house and outside in the biomes of [[Dreamlight Valley]]. 

Furniture is bought from {{il|Scrooge McDuck}} at his [[Scrooge’s Store|Store]], there is a set of basic furniture which can always be ordered by speaking to {{il|Scrooge McDuck}} while in the Store, other Furniture items appear on a daily rotation, Furniture items can be ordered directly once bought in the store.

Some Furniture items are exclusive to [[quests]] and [[Friendship]] rewards. Exclusive rewards that have already been obtained can be ordered from {{il|Scrooge McDuck}}.

Furniture items are logged in the ''Furniture Sets'' collection once obtained, with the exception of some random Furniture, and crafted Furniture which are listed in the ''Crafting'' collection.

: {{icon|Crafting| 2}} - Furniture that can be crafted
: {{icon|Star Path| 2}} - Furniture that is obtained from the [[Star Path]]
