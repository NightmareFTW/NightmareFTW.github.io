'''Companions''' are equipable pets which follow the player around. They can be equipped from the Wardrobe menu in the Inventory when the player is outside. They can be interacted with to pet them, doing so emits a heart above the companion's head.

Companions are obtained through various methods, including feeding [[critters]], completing [[quests]], and redeeming [[Star Path]] tokens.
== Critters ==
:''Main article: [[Critters]]''
Critters are wild animals that roam biomes and can be fed, each biome has a different type of critter. Feeding a critter multiple times will unlock it as a Companion.
