'''Clothing''' are wearable items can be equipped from the Wardrobe to customise the player’s avatar. They can be bought or rewarded from missions and the [[Star Path]].  

Once obtained, Clothing items are logged in the ''Clothing Sets'' collection in the Collection menu.  

==List of Clothing==
: {{icon|Star Path| 2}} - Clothing that is obtained from the [[Star Path]].
: {{icon|Friendship| 2}} - Clothing that is rewarded for levelling up [[Friendship]] with [[characters|villagers]].
: {{icon|Dreamlight Duties| 2}} - Clothing that is rewarded for completing time-limited [[Dreamlight Duties]].
: {{icon|Founder's Pack| 2}} - Clothing that is obtained from [[Founder’s Pack]]s.
