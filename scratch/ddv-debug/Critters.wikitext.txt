'''Critters''' in [[Dreamlight Valley]] are animals that can interacted with and obtained as [[:Category:Companions|companions]]. Each biome has a unique Critter species to feed and befriend in different color variations. Critters is also a collection, interacting with and obtaining critters logs them in the Critters collection.

==Interacting==
Critters can be interacted with when close to them, each critter require a unique method of approaching and interacting. Feeding a Critter a food that it likes multiple times will recruit the Critter as companion, although the exact number of times for any critter has yet to be determined. Feeding Critters only counts once a day, as seen by the grey symbol with a tick. Critters will drop items when fed food they like.
